# China Mobile

> China Mobile Limited is the world's largest mobile network operator by subscribers — about 1,005 million mobile customers and 110 million gigabit broadband customers across all 31 provinces of mainland China plus Hong Kong. In the network-API value chain it sits on the operator side: a GSMA Open Gateway member since June 2023, the sponsor of nine CAMARA APIs, and the holder of a GSMA Open Gateway certification for its Network-as-a-Service platform. Almost none of that is callable from the open internet. Its self-serve, publicly documented API surface is OneNET, the IoT platform operated by its CMIOT subsidiary.

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-07-25 from the artifacts in this repository. China Mobile publishes no llms.txt of its own (probed: open.iot.10086.cn, api.iot.10086.cn, dev.10086.cn, www.chinamobileltd.com — all miss).

## What is actually callable

- [OneNET Studio application API](https://open.iot.10086.cn/doc/iot_platform/book/api/introduce.html): action-dispatched gateway at `https://openapi.heclouds.com/{namespace}?action={Action}&version=1`. Namespaces `application`, `common`, `lwm2m-online`, `lwm2m-offline`. 44 operations documented — devices, products, projects, groups, thing models, properties, services, files, scene-linkage rules.
- [OneNET Voice Call Service](https://open.iot.10086.cn/doc/iot_platform/book/vcs/vcs_api/request.html): `https://openapi.heclouds.com/vcs?action={voiceNotify|dialNotify}&version=2`. Click to dial and TTS voice notification, with call-status callbacks. This is the first-party product behind the CAMARA Click to Dial API China Mobile sponsors.
- [OneNET legacy device cloud API](https://open.iot.10086.cn/doc/multiprotocol/): `https://api.heclouds.com` — live, token-authenticated, `{"errno":3,"error":"token鉴权失败"}` to anonymous callers.
- [IoT Card Capability Open Platform](https://api.iot.10086.cn/apiDocuments/cusInfo/codeInfoQuery.html): `https://api.iot.10086.cn/v2/cardinfo` (CMIOT_API2003) resolves ICCID, IMSI or MSISDN into the other two. Credentials issued by email to approved enterprise IoT-card customers only.

## What is documented but gated

- [Communication Capability Open Platform](https://ct.open.10086.cn/portal/developer/docAndHelp.action): 语音通知, 语音验证码, 点击拨号, 中间号 (number masking), QoS保障. Products named, no anonymous endpoint reference.
- [Internet Capability Open Platform](https://dev.10086.cn/): 移动认证/号码认证 (number authentication via CMPassport), 大数据服务, 通信能力, 支付能力. An enterprise procurement console, not a developer portal.

## Things an agent should know before calling

- Every response is **HTTP 200**. Branch on the `success` field, never on the status code.
- There is **no idempotency key** anywhere in the estate. Retrying `CallService`, `dialNotify` or `voiceNotify` re-executes — the voice actions place a second billable telephone call.
- There are **no published rate limits**, no 429 semantics, and no status page.
- The console and all documentation are **Chinese-language**, and full entitlement requires 实名认证 (real-name verification). Everything outside OneNET requires an enterprise contract and manually issued credentials.
- China Mobile is **not** an Aduna shareholder; it reaches developers through its own domestic capability marketplaces rather than the global aggregator channel.
